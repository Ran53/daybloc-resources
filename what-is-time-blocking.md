# What is Time Blocking?

Time blocking is a time management method where you divide your day into distinct blocks of time, assigning a specific task or category of work to each block. Instead of working from a list of tasks and hoping to get them done, you schedule them into your calendar like appointments.

## Why it works
To-do lists are infinite. Time is finite. Time blocking forces you to confront the reality of your day. By giving every task a physical space on a timeline, you eliminate decision fatigue and protect your focus.

## Core Principles
1. **Give everything a time:** If it's not on the timeline, it's not a plan. 
2. **Batch similar tasks:** Group administrative work, emails, and meetings into dedicated blocks.
3. **Protect deep work:** Schedule your most cognitively demanding tasks during your peak energy hours.
4. **Leave buffer time:** Things take longer than expected. Build in transition time between blocks.
