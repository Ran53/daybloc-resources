# How to Time Block Your Day

Implementing time blocking is straightforward, but it takes practice to get right. Here is a step-by-step guide to building your first time-blocked day.

## 1. Brain Dump
Before you plan your day, list everything you need to do. Don't worry about order yet.

## 2. Estimate Time
Assign a realistic time estimate to each task. Be honest—most people underestimate how long tasks take. 

## 3. Map out the Timeline
Open your time-blocking tool. Start by placing fixed commitments (meetings, calls, appointments) on the timeline first.

## 4. Assign Priorities
Place your most important tasks (often called "deep work") during the hours when you have the most energy. Use colors to differentiate between types of work (e.g., Red for deep work, Blue for admin, Green for personal time).

## 5. Handle the Unexpected
Your plan will rarely go perfectly. When an interruption happens, simply drag the affected block to a new time. The goal is not a perfect day; it is having a clear direction.

## 6. Review and Adjust
At the end of the day, look at what you planned versus what you actually accomplished. This feedback loop is crucial for improving your estimates.
