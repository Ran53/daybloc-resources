# The Importance of Offline Time Blocking

In an era where every productivity app requires an account, a cloud sync, and an internet connection, the concept of a local-first, offline planner is becoming rare.

## Why Privacy Matters in Planning
Your daily schedule reveals a lot about you. It shows your health habits, your work stress, your personal priorities, and your free time. Many users do not want this data synced to a cloud server or used for analytics.

## The Benefits of an Offline App
1. **Speed:** There is no network latency. Dragging a block on DayBloc is instantaneous because it is reading and writing to the device's local storage.
2. **Reliability:** You can plan your day on an airplane, on the subway, or during a power outage.
3. **No Account Friction:** You download the app and start planning. No email verification, no password to remember.

## DayBloc's Approach
DayBloc is built as an offline-first app. All your time blocks, routines, and streak data are stored locally on your device. While we do have a support chat, the core planning experience never requires an internet connection.
