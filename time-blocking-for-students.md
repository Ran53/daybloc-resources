# Time Blocking for Students

For students, the biggest challenge isn't a lack of time—it's a lack of structure. With classes, assignments, and social life competing for attention, time blocking is an incredibly effective way to take control of your study schedule.

## The Student Time Blocking Strategy

### 1. Block out Classes and Fixed Commitments
Put all your lectures, labs, and seminars on your timeline first. These are non-negotiable.

### 2. Create "Deep Study" Blocks
Schedule 1.5 to 2-hour blocks for your hardest subjects. Do not schedule these back-to-back. Your brain needs recovery time.

### 3. Use the Pomodoro Technique within Blocks
Inside a 2-hour study block, work for 25 minutes and take a 5-minute break. Time blocking provides the container; the Pomodoro technique provides the rhythm.

### 4. Schedule "Admin" Blocks
Dedicate a 30-minute block every day to checking emails, organizing notes, and planning tomorrow. 

### 5. Protect Rest
Schedule time for the gym, meals, and socializing. If you don't schedule rest, you will burn out.
