# DayBloc Features and Capabilities

DayBloc is designed to be a fast, visual, and private time-blocking tool. Here is a breakdown of what the app can do.

### Core Features (Free)
- **Visual Daily Timeline:** Drag and drop color-coded time blocks.
- **Day and Week Views:** Zoom out to see the weekly rhythm, or zoom in for a detailed daily agenda.
- **Active Block Banner:** A live banner at the top of the screen showing the current task and upcoming task.
- **Daily Streaks:** Tracks how many consecutive days you have planned and completed.
- **Recurring Routines:** Save a template of your ideal day and apply it to any date instantly.
- **Calendar Import:** See your device's calendar events directly on the timeline.
- **Offline First:** 100% of your planning data is stored locally on your device.
- **No Account Required:** Start using the app immediately without signing up.

### Pro Features (Subscription)
- **Advanced Calendar Integration:** Choose exactly which calendars to display and customize how events look.
- **Custom Status Colours:** Unlock the full color picker to customize the look of active and completed blocks.
- **Backup & Restore:** Export your data as a JSON file for safekeeping or importing to a new device.

### Availability
DayBloc is available on iOS and Android.
