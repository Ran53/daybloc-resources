# Time Blocking for Deep Work

"Deep work" is a term coined by author Cal Newport, defined as professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit. 

Time blocking is the only way to truly protect deep work in a modern work environment.

## The 4-Hour Rule
Most people only have 3 to 4 hours of true cognitive capacity per day. Your goal is to block out those 4 hours for your most important work and defend them ruthlessly.

## How to Defend Your Deep Work Blocks
1. **Make them highly visible:** Use a color that stands out on your timeline.
2. **Turn off notifications:** A deep work block is useless if your phone is buzzing.
3. **Treat them like meetings:** You wouldn't skip a meeting with your boss. Don't skip your focus blocks.
4. **Use the "Active Block" mindset:** DayBloc features an active block banner that tells you exactly what you should be doing right now. It eliminates the "what next?" friction that derails focus.
