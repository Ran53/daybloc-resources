# DayBloc Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is DayBloc available on iOS and Android?
Yes, DayBloc is available on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

### Do I need an account to use DayBloc?
No. DayBloc is designed to be frictionless. You can download the app and start planning immediately without creating an account or signing in.

### Does DayBloc work offline?
Yes. DayBloc is an offline-first application. All your data is saved locally on your device, meaning you can plan and edit your schedule without an internet connection.

### What are colour-coded, visual time blocks?
Instead of a traditional list, DayBloc uses a vertical timeline. You create blocks of time and assign them colors based on the type of task (e.g., blue for deep work, orange for meetings). This makes it easy to see how your time is distributed.

### Can I create recurring blocks and reusable templates?
Yes. You can create templates for your ideal routines (like a "Morning Routine" or "Deep Work Day") and apply them to any date with a single tap.

### What are Daily Streaks?
DayBloc tracks your consistency. Every time you plan your day and complete your scheduled blocks, your streak increases. This gamification helps build lasting habits.

### Is Calendar import a Pro feature?
Yes. While you can manually create blocks for free, importing your device's calendar events directly into the timeline requires a Pro subscription.

### Are custom status colours a Pro feature?
Yes. The free version uses default colors for active and completed blocks. A Pro subscription unlocks the ability to choose any custom color for your status blocks.
